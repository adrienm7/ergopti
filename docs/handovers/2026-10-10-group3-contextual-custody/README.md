# Linux contextual callback custody

The deferred editor shortcut checked admission before logging, but the real
shared logger synchronously invokes its configured sink. That callback could
change the live gates, source, binding or configuration owner before executor
entry. The received repair retains the original decision/admission issuers and
checks their custody again after callbacks and immediately before execution.
Getter exceptions refuse delivery. Ordinary and physical shortcut paths retain
their existing behavior.

Fresh serial replay on LuaJIT 2.1.1737090214 and Lua 5.4.7 qualifies the bounded
component change:

- Each candidate runs all 20 new cases successfully; the original source passes
  one and fails 19.
- Seven independent one-span inverses fail their exact frozen causal cases on
  both ABIs, with 19, 3, 2, 1, 1, 4 and 1 failures respectively.
- All 14 original editor and three configuration-owner cases pass against both
  source images on both ABIs. The whole original enrolled test remains unchanged.
- Five enrollment controls refuse zero, mistyped, missing, foreign and duplicate
  selections on each ABI. Successful zero-test selection is impossible.
- All 2,119 preparation dependency hashes match before and after replay.

The independent source and receiving reviews are included. These fixtures do
not qualify native input/output custody, installed startup, packages, physical
editor-key delivery or the remaining collision/retargeting scope of item 108.
Four original editor runs emit their unchanged installed-startup refusal;
those lines are retained as refusals, not startup successes.

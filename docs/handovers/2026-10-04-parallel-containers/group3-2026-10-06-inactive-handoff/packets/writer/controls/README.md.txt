## Implemented native contract

A constructor/binder reserves the writer before lazy FFI loading, symbol lookup, provider callbacks or descriptor acquisition. Nested opens, backend replacement, reset, capture and writes are refused. Native syscall symbols are captured while reserved. Constructor capabilities require literal ioctl acknowledgements and a nonnegative signed-int descriptor; historical truthy ordinary open behavior remains, but cannot mint forced-output authority. Constructor rollback retains exact unknown-close ownership and never retries a possibly recycled fd.

capture_output() issues an opaque per-open token only before any untracked legacy wire attempt; no fd or backend is exposed. output_current()/output_view() are pure observed checks of exact identity and immutable native ports, not kernel leases. Detached views contain generation and write_epoch only. The private wire implementation accepts only exact capability identity and valid native event scalars, requires literal KEY and SYN acknowledgements, and advances one epoch per attempted whole pair. Previously captured ordinary emitters delegate the same private transport after capture, so external same-channel emissions remain observable by the future ledger rather than bypassing epochs. No held-modifier or physical-origin authority is inferred from these transport receipts.

close_owned() requires the exact captured native retirement and close acknowledgements. Unknown or throwing close retains the owner/debt and never retries a numeric fd; write debt can retire a still-known exact open channel once. Backend metatables and replaced public current/encoder/ffi exports cannot silently substitute the issued private authority.

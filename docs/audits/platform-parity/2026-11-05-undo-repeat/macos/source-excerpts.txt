366: ### project-the-bubble-never-offers-a-doubling
367: 
368: The maintainer ruled that no driver previews the magic key's doubling (the
369: repeat fallback, `x★` → `xx`): it is available after almost every mid-word
370: letter, so its row was constant noise. It still fires. Windows keeps it in
371: `HSE_PreviewNextDecision`, so no lower candidate is promoted in its place, and
372: `_PrefixCollectCandidates` withholds any decision whose Spec carries
373: `IsRepeat`. macOS and Linux preview only registry mappings, and the doubling
374: is an engine fallback outside the registry: never fold it into their preview.
375: `repeat_corrections` entries (ê → u after a doubled letter) are not doublings.

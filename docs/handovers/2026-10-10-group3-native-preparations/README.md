## Verify and recover

extracting or executing retained source:

```sh
sha256sum -c files.sha256
python3 build_archive.py --verify
```

`native-preparations.tar.gz` contains only inert regular `.txt` members with short
portable names. `members.json` maps each member to its exact original packet,
relative filename, source mode, byte count and SHA256. Tar ownership/timestamps
are normalized to zero and member permissions to read-only. The gzip timestamp
is zero. The generator compares two serializations and rereads all inputs before
publication; identical inventory and source bytes reproduce the archive.

To inspect a source, extract its mapped inert member into a fresh owned scratch
directory. Verify its member hash before restoring its original name and mode.
Do not apply patches, run retained programs or restore absolute historical paths
automatically. Original receipts may refer to private paths, current source,
native libraries or excluded archives; those dependencies are not made portable
by this capsule. Excluded bytes cannot be reconstructed from their hashes.
